FAQ

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, support@pkmnmasterset.com reaches a human.

The tracker

Do I need an account just to look at cards?

No — the whole library is browsable without one. An account is what lets the app remember which cards are yours.

Is my collection data mine?

Yes. Export it as JSON whenever you like, on the free plan too. Your collection is keyed to card IDs, so it stays meaningful anywhere.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — it installs from the browser like an app, and visited pages keep working offline.

Can I track 1st Editions, holos and stamps separately?

That's the whole point: every printing is its own tick with its own count, and custom printings can be added for the oddballs.

Premium & payments

What does Master Set Premium cost, and what's in it?

$2.99/month: unlimited binders now, the card scanner when it ships. The free account keeps full collection tracking and one binder, forever.

What happens to my binders if I cancel?

Your data is never deleted. Your first binder stays fully usable; binders beyond it lock — covers visible, contents kept — until you're Premium again. Everything comes back exactly as you left it.

How do payments work?

Through Stripe — card details never touch our servers. Cancel any time from Manage subscription on your Account page; your plan stays active through the period you've paid for.

The API

How do I get an API token?

Pick a plan on the API docs page — your token is minted the moment you subscribe, shown once. For the free tier (1,000 requests/mo), email support@pkmnmasterset.com.

Do card images cost API requests?

No. Images cost 0 against every plan, permanently — that's a standing policy of this project.

I lost my token.

Tokens are shown once and only their fingerprint is stored, so it can't be re-displayed. Email support: the old one gets revoked, a new one minted.

The project

Is this affiliated with The Pokémon Company?

No. This is an independent fan-built tool. Card names and data are used descriptively to identify cards; no card artwork is sold, ever.

Who runs this?

A collector who wanted a proper master-set tracker and ended up building the whole thing — tracker, API, and all. Small by design; email actually gets read.
