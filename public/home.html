Fair questions

What happens to my binders if I cancel Premium?

Nothing. You keep every binder you ever made, can open and edit them all, and simply can't create more than one new one until you upgrade again. Nothing is ever deleted or locked over money.

Is my collection data mine?

Yes. Export it as JSON whenever you like, on the free plan too. Your collection is keyed to card IDs, so it stays meaningful anywhere.

Do I need an account just to look at cards?

No — the whole library is browsable without one. An account is what lets the app remember which cards are yours.

How do payments work?

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe — the card details never touch our servers. Cancel any time from the payment receipt's manage link; your plan stays active through the period you've paid for.
